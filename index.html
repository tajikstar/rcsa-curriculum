K-12 Journey

Your Child's Path at RCSA

Each stage builds on the last, from early curiosity to college-level work.

K-5

Building the STEM Foundation

Students build strong reading, writing and math skills while exploring science through hands-on projects. Writing is part of every math and science block, so students learn to explain their thinking. In our Innovation Labs, students practice coding and engineering through building, circuits and design challenges.

  • Integrated literacy and STEM
  • Innovation Labs (iLabs)
  • Hands-on, project-based learning
  • Gifted education
  • Character education
6-8

The Acceleration Bridge

Middle school students take on advanced coursework and can begin earning high school credit. Through our gifted enrichment model, students are included in advanced cohorts.

  • Electives in technology, foreign languages and fine arts, plus Speech and Debate and Model U.N.
  • Science Olympiad, Math Olympiad and Robotics
  • Gifted enrichment with advanced cohorts
  • High school credit: Algebra I, Geometry, Biology, Foreign Language
  • Pre-Early College
  • Character education
9-12

Pathways to College

High school students choose a rigorous path that fits their goals, from AP research to earning an AA degree before graduation. Every student is supported by the College Mentorship Program.

  • Advanced Placement (AP) and AP Capstone
  • Early College (AA degree)
  • Elite Scholars Program (ESP)
  • College Mentorship Program (CMP)
  • Electives: Computer Science, Robotics, Web Design, Creative Writing, Psychology, Spanish, Turkish
  • Character education

Program offerings may vary by campus based on student interest and availability. Contact your school for details.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an entrance exam?

No. As a public charter school, RCSA admits students by lottery. After enrollment, placement testing helps place each student in the right level.

How can my child earn college credit?

Middle school students can begin with Pre-Early College. High school students can take AP courses and exams, and are able to earn an AA degree through Early College.

Can middle school students earn high school credit?

Yes. Qualified middle school students can earn high school credit in Algebra I, Geometry, Biology and Foreign Language.

What extra help is available?

Teachers hold Student Hours for personalized help. Times vary by campus, so check with your school.

How does RCSA support student well-being?

Along with academic support, RCSA provides school counseling and wellness resources so students stay healthy while they take on challenging work. Our character education program challenges students to be their best in and out of the classroom.

Is transportation provided?

Busing is available only at designated hubs to and from the high school campus. Families are responsible for all other transportation to and from school. Transportation is provided for off-site field trips. Contact your campus for details specific to your student.

How much homework should we expect?

About 15-40 minutes a night in grades K-2, 45-90 minutes in grades 3-7, and up to 3 hours in grade 8 and above, depending on the courses taken.
